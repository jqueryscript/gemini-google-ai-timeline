# Google AI Timeline

A public timeline of major Google AI, Gemini, DeepMind, Veo, Imagen, Gemma, and developer platform milestones.

This repository is built as a clean reference for readers, developers, researchers, writers, and AI builders who need a quick way to track Google's AI release history. It covers early Google Search machine learning, Google Translate, TensorFlow, AlphaGo, the Transformer, BERT, AlphaFold, Bard, Gemini, Gemma, Imagen, Nano Banana, Veo, NotebookLM, Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, and Gemini Intelligence.

Last updated: May 14, 2026

## Full Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
|---:|---|
| 2001 | Machine learning helped Google Search suggest better spellings for user queries. |
| 2006 | Google Translate launched and made machine translation available to a broad public audience. |
| 2015 | Google released TensorFlow as an open-source machine learning framework. |
| 2016 | AlphaGo defeated world champion Lee Sedol in Go, a major public milestone for DeepMind. |
| 2016 | Google's Tensor Processing Units helped accelerate large-scale AI workloads. |
| 2017 | Google researchers introduced the Transformer architecture, which became a foundation for modern language models. |
| 2019 | BERT helped Google Search better understand the meaning and context of queries. |
| 2020 | AlphaFold made a major breakthrough in protein structure prediction. |
| 2023 | Bard launched as Google's generative AI assistant. |
| May 2023 | Google introduced PaLM 2 and expanded generative AI features across Search, Workspace, and Labs. |
| August 2023 | Google launched Duet AI for Google Workspace and expanded Duet AI in Google Cloud. |
| December 6, 2023 | Google introduced Gemini 1.0 with Ultra, Pro, and Nano model sizes. |
| February 2024 | Bard was renamed Gemini, and the Gemini app began rolling out on Android and iOS through the Google app. |
| February 2024 | Google introduced Gemini 1.5 with major long-context improvements. |
| February 2024 | Google released Gemma, a family of open models built from the same research and technology used for Gemini. |
| May 14, 2024 | Google introduced Gemini 1.5 Pro improvements and Gemini 1.5 Flash for faster multimodal use. |
| May 14, 2024 | Google introduced VideoFX, plus new updates for ImageFX and MusicFX. |
| September 2024 | NotebookLM added Audio Overviews, which turn uploaded files into audio discussions. |
| December 11, 2024 | Google introduced Gemini 2.0 Flash with stronger multimodal capabilities and native tool use. |
| March 25, 2025 | Google introduced Gemini 2.5 Pro as an experimental model with stronger reasoning and coding. |
| April 9, 2025 | Google introduced Agent2Agent, an open protocol for agent interoperability. |
| May 20, 2025 | Google I/O 2025 brought major updates including Flow, Veo 3, Imagen 4, AI Mode, Deep Think, Gemma 3n, Stitch, and Gemini Code Assist. |
| October 15, 2025 | Google introduced Veo 3.1 and advanced Flow capabilities with richer audio and more video control. |
| November 18, 2025 | Google introduced Gemini 3 and Gemini 3 Pro in preview. |
| November 18, 2025 | Google announced Antigravity, an agentic development platform for AI-assisted software work. |
| December 17, 2025 | Google released Gemini 3 Flash for faster everyday AI use and developer workflows. |
| February 19, 2026 | Google released Gemini 3.1 Pro for complex tasks and agent workflows. |
| February 26, 2026 | Google introduced Nano Banana 2, also known as Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, for faster image generation and editing across Google products. |
| March 31, 2026 | Google introduced Veo 3.1 Lite as its most cost-effective video generation model. |
| April 2, 2026 | Google introduced Gemma 4, its most capable Gemma open model family to date. |
| April 22, 2026 | Google launched Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and made Gemini Embedding 2 generally available. |
| May 7, 2026 | Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite became generally available for enterprise production workloads. |
